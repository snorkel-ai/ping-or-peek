ordinary prose count without a question mark. Ordinary acknowledgments, progress reports, and

reply does not imply the assistant asked.
checks_in: the message contains a specific question, invitation to correct the selected file,
or offer to revise.

anything) count as neither asking nor checking in about this task.

listed here and are reported separately.
